Our mission

Asheville is unlike anywhere else. It's a city of artists, musicians, entrepreneurs, hikers, volunteers, craft brewers, makers, and neighbors who value creativity, kindness, and community. That spirit deserves a place where people can easily find one another and share real experiences.

JoinIn was created in Asheville, for Asheville. We're not a distant tech company or a global social platform optimized for screen time. We're a local company with a simple goal: to strengthen the community we call home.

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life. JoinIn isn't pro-AI or anti-AI — we simply believe that as technology grows more present, real human connection becomes more important. That's why JoinIn is human-first by design: every feature is built to help people meet face-to-face. You'll find neighbors instead of bots, conversations instead of algorithms, and experiences that happen in the real world.

We also believe community access should be open. That's why JoinIn is free for everyone — hosts and members alike — so we can focus on real connections instead of paywalls, starting in places like Asheville and growing outward.

Because the future shouldn't only be smarter. It should be more human.

Meet the founder

Marc Postlewaite

Journalist · Author of Origin 2.0 · Entrepreneur

Marc has spent his career doing two things: turning complicated subjects into clear, human stories — and building things meant to last.

He started in the newsroom, reporting and editing for The Knoxville Journal, The Mountain Press, the Chicago Tribune Company, and Harte Hanks Communications. Those years gave him a reporter's instinct for the local story and an editor's eye for what a community actually wants to read — the exact instincts a JoinIn Publisher builds a city on.

He went on to become an author and self-described “science entrepreneur,” writing Origin 2.0: From the Big Bang to the Future of Humanity — a sweeping, accessible history of the universe (reviewers put it alongside Carl Sagan and Sapiens) that lands on a single idea: every human story is part of a much larger whole, and connection is what makes us human.

That idea is the seed of JoinIn. Origin 2.0 ends on a hard question — how do we stay connected and human as AI reshapes daily life? JoinIn is his answer at street level: technology that puts neighbors back in the same room instead of deeper into a screen.

A reporter's nose for the local story, an author's clarity, and an entrepreneur's drive to build it — that's what JoinIn runs on, and what Marc brings to every Publisher he works with.

How JoinIn works

Built to bring people together

Every part of JoinIn is designed around one idea: making it effortless to take part. The whole app leans toward participation and inclusion — it’s free, it’s local, and it helps anyone find their people and actually show up. Here’s how the pieces fit, each flowing naturally into the next:

1
See what’s going on
At a glance, everything happening in — today, this week, or a date range you choose. The front door to the whole city.
2
Community Columns
Local writers keep you up to date on the neighborhoods and communities you care about.
3
JoinIn & Participate
The heart of it — a Meetup-style space where anyone can join a group, class, or event, or start one. Always free for hosts and guests alike, and built for a single purpose: bringing people together in person.
4
Pulse of
A Facebook-style feed where neighbors share what they’re into, ask questions, and jump into the conversation.
5
Today’s Topics
A topic of the day and an poll — so the whole city can weigh in on what matters.
6
Marketplace
A friendly local board to buy, sell, and give things away free — kept neighbor to neighbor.

See it → follow it → join it → talk about it. Every step gently moves people off the screen and into the same room.

The revenue engine

How businesses advertise on JoinIn

Local businesses reach people who are already looking for things to do — no algorithm, no chasing. It's self-serve: pick an ad type, set a budget, and it runs. This is what funds the free platform, and most of it stays local.

Pulse ads

Sponsored posts in the local feed, with map + website buttons. Run 1–7 days.

Column sponsorships

A business sponsors a neighborhood or topic column — trusted, local placement.

Marketplace & boosts

Optional paid boosts for extra visibility. Classifieds stay free. (Banner ads later.)

Best of all, it’s automated — Publishers never sell. The platform handles ad sales on its own, and 80% of every dollar stays local (Publisher 55% · columnists 25%).

The honest part

Leadership first — ownership that grows

Let’s be straight about money, because you deserve honesty. JoinIn is a brand-new venture in , and like anything worth building, it starts small. In the first months, expect little to no income — this is about planting something, not cashing a check. The real reward at the start is the one you already value: being a recognized leader in your community.

1
You own it
This isn’t a job — it’s your business. You own the franchise as an asset: something you build, grow, and — under the terms of the agreement — may one day sell to a new publisher.
2
You don’t sell ads
You’re not a salesperson. As JoinIn grows, revenue comes from automated ad sales built into the platform. You focus on the community; the selling takes care of itself.
3
Small now — meaningful later
Expect modest returns early. As your city fills with real groups, columns, and daily readers, it grows — and over the years it can become significant.
