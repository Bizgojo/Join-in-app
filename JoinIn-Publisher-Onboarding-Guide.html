Your main job

Onboarding columnists

Your biggest lever is recruiting great columnists. There are two kinds:

Neighborhood columnist \u00b7 paid

Covers one neighborhood and earns a share of the ad revenue on their column. You recruit them the same way you were recruited.

Topic columnist \u00b7 volunteer

Covers a passion topic (food, trails, music). Unpaid, but a great on-ramp and a source of steady content.

We give you the scripts, the outreach steps, and an interactive guide to send to your own candidates.
